KitchenMate: Solving Choice Overload

Product Strategy · Gemini 1.5 Pro · UX Design
The Friction: "What's for Dinner?"
Urban households face structural decision fatigue. While 57% of households report daily stress regarding meal choices, most apps fail because manual inventory tracking feels like a chore. This leads to "takeout-by-default" and contributes to a massive $400B annual food waste problem.

Choice Overload: Information from cookbooks and YouTube increases cognitive effort rather than reducing it. KitchenMate moves from "planning" to "action."
Target Audience
💼
Depleted Rahul
Professional, 28
Low mental bandwidth after work; needs a zero-thought solution that uses what's in the fridge.
👪
Efficient Ananya
Busy Parent, 35
Needs to feed a family quickly without manual inventory checking.
Product Strategy: Hick's Law
The core strategy centers on minimizing Choice Paralysis. By limiting meal suggestions to exactly three curated options, we shift the user's mental model from a complex search problem to a simple 30-second decision.

North Star Metric: Time-to-First-Decision (TTFD). Our goal is to move users from "opening app" to "selecting meal" in **< 30 seconds**.
