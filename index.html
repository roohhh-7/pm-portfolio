Project Case Study

AI · Consumer Health · Personal Project · 0→1
Problem Statement
Nutrition labels are information-heavy but decision-poor. Consumers face a wall of numbers — calories, macros, ingredient lists — with no clear signal on whether a product is suitable for them. The gap isn't data; it's interpretation. Even health-conscious shoppers hesitate at the shelf because translating nutritional data into a personal judgement call requires knowledge most people don't have.
User Personas
🏋️
Fitness Farhan
Gym-goer, 28
Tracks macros, wants quick protein-to-calorie ratios without reading every label
"I can't tell if this is actually high-protein or just marketed that way."
👩‍💼
Busy Priya
Working professional, 34
Wants fast, trustworthy decisions while grocery shopping with limited time
"I have 2 minutes in the aisle — I just need a yes or no."
🧓
Health-aware Meena
Parent, 45 — managing family diet
Monitoring sodium & sugar for a family member with diabetes
"I never know which number to look at first."
Solution & How it works
Smart Food Label Checker uses computer vision + AI reasoning to extract nutritional data from a label photo and deliver a structured health verdict in under 5 seconds.
